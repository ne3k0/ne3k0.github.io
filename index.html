Nico García-Peguinho

Nico is a UK based musician and researcher. He's currently a PhD student in Sound & Music Computing at the Centre for Digital Music [C4DM], Queen Mary University of London. His research revolves around interpretable and eXplainable AI [XAI] methods for audio neural networks, with a particular interest in exploring their internal mechanics and how these networks may be subverted for critical, creative and experimental ends. Outside of his research, he builds music technology and makes/performs music [Ze Carinho | Ne3k0].
